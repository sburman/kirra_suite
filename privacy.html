Your privacy

Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal information, in plain language.

Last updated 18 July 2026.

This policy explains how The Kirra Suite ("we", "us") handles personal information in connection with this website, kirrasuite.com, and enquiries or bookings for our beachfront apartment at Marine Parade, Kirra Beach QLD 4225. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

The short version

This website is an information site. It does not have its own booking form, login, or account system, and we do not sell your personal information. When you choose to book, you are taken to our property manager's booking platform, which collects the details needed to process your reservation under its own privacy policy.

Information we collect

  • Booking and enquiry details. If you book or contact us, you may provide your name, email, phone number, stay dates, guest numbers, and payment details. These are collected and processed by our property manager and the booking channel you use, not stored by this website.
  • Basic technical data. Like most websites, our host records standard server information such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you view. This helps keep the site running and secure.

How we use your information

  • To respond to enquiries and confirm, manage, and service your stay.
  • To meet legal, tax, and safety obligations connected to short-stay accommodation.
  • To understand how the website is used and to improve it.
  • To promote The Kirra Suite, including through advertising on social platforms.

Cookies and analytics

This site aims to stay light and does not run its own advertising or tracking cookies. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers to fetch them. Your browser settings let you block or clear cookies at any time.

Services we rely on

We use a small number of trusted third parties, each with their own privacy terms:

  • Our property manager (POD Property Management) processes direct bookings and guest communication.
  • Airbnb and Booking.com, if you book through those channels, handle your details under their own policies.
  • Meta (Facebook and Instagram), which we use to advertise The Kirra Suite. Advertising is targeted using interests and location rather than personal details you give us, and any reporting we see is aggregated. We do not currently run a tracking pixel on this website.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website and processes basic technical data as described above.

How long we keep it

Booking records are kept only as long as needed to service your stay and to meet legal and tax obligations, then securely removed. Technical logs are kept for short periods for security and troubleshooting.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, subject to any records we are legally required to keep. You can also opt out of personalised ads through your Facebook and Instagram ad settings.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows when it last changed.

Contact us

For any privacy question or request, contact us at steven.burman@gmail.com.
